Overview

My Password Vault is a password manager that feels as simple as a spreadsheet – easy to scan, easy to edit, and intuitive from day one. Designed and built end-to-end for web, iOS, and Android.

Easy and intuitive

  • Quick search and categories to stay organized
  • Copy passwords when you need them
  • Works on phone and web with the same account
  • Free to start – upgrade only when you need more entries

Privacy first

No ads in the vault. Personal information is not sold. Sign in with Google, Apple, or email. Encrypted on-device; unlock with a passkey, with TOTP and recovery codes as backup. Only ciphertext syncs to the server.

Key ideas

  • Spreadsheet-simple – see logins in a clean table: site, username, password, notes, and categories
  • Secure without friction – encryption and unlock that stay out of the way
  • Free, then one payment – start with 25 entries; unlock unlimited with a one-time Pro license (no subscription)
