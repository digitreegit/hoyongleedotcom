O2 / Telefónica – Mobile Wallet App

Mobile payment app for O2/Telefónica – Germany's 3rd largest mobile network operator. Concept aligned with E-Plus BASE wallet.

Overview

Mobile payment app for O2/Telefónica Germany. Similar concept to the E-Plus BASE wallet, the goal was to deliver a complete enterprise mobile app and platform for O2's customer base.

Project was eventually cancelled following the corporate migration with E-Plus, but the design system, generic flow and payment flow were fully delivered.

Deliverables

  • Generic flow (full customer journey)
  • Payment flow (NFC tap & go)
  • Screen design and asset documentation
