Overview

Mobile payment app for E-Plus, the 2nd largest mobile network operator in Germany. Payment via NFC tap & go, coupon and membership card via barcode scan, and additional consumer-friendly features.

Implemented as an enterprise mobile app and platform — commercial launch in July 2014, Germany (Android).

Key Features

  • NFC-based tap & go payment
  • Coupon and membership card (barcode scan)
  • Loyalty & promotion engine
  • Enterprise mobile platform & admin tools
