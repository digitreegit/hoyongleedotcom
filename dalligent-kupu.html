KUPU — Mobile Recruitment Platform

Skill-based mobile recruitment platform that connects business owners and HR professionals with the right candidates, powered by an AI-ready core engine.

Role

Overview

KUPU is a skill-based mobile recruitment platform that connects business owners and HR professionals with the right candidates. Led business concept, branding, UI/UX, wireframe/wireflow, and design from 0 to launch.

The platform is powered by an AI-ready core engine that matches job providers and seekers with precision. Commercially launched in Indonesia in 2021.

Design System

Built on Atomic Design methodology with a Figma token-based system. Consistent component library across iOS and Android surfaces, enabling rapid iteration and scalable design.

Key Features

  • Skill-based job matching powered by AI-ready engine
  • Comprehensive mobile solution for business owners & HR professionals
  • Job seeker profile with skill verification
  • In-app messaging & interview scheduling
  • Analytics dashboard for recruiters
